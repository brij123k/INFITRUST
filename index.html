About Us

Skilled craftsmanship, delivered worldwide

Infitrust International Private Limited is a trusted jewellery sourcing and manufacturing partner from India, serving private-label brands, designers, retailers and wholesalers across 25+ countries.

Our key product categories include 925 Sterling Silver with Lab-Grown Diamonds, Gold Jewellery with Lab-Grown Diamonds, Demi-Fine Jewellery, Micron-Plated Jewellery, Iced-Out & Hip-Hop Jewellery, and Gemstone & Fashion Jewellery.

We work with trusted manufacturers and audited factories, combining skilled craftsmanship with stringent quality control, competitive pricing, low MOQs and reliable delivery for international markets.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

How can we trust the quality of your silver jewellery?

Our jewellery is made in 925 sterling silver following the highest international quality standards, since we export all over the world. Our silver jewellery can be certified by top credible independent testing agencies like BIS (Bureau of Indian Standards) as per the client's request. We also remain in constant touch with the customer by providing photographs and videos of the product, ensuring complete customer satisfaction.

How can we trust the quality of your diamond jewellery?

Our jewellery is made by skilled artisans and we have a thorough quality check process. We certify our jewellery by top credible independent testing agencies like IGI or GIA as per the client's request. We also remain in constant touch with the customer by providing photographs and videos of the product, ensuring complete customer satisfaction.

Do you accept custom designs (OEM/ODM)?

Yes — we highly specialise in custom design manufacturing services and welcome OEM and ODM. We will develop your design and it can be customised for the base metal (14K gold, 18K gold, white gold, rose gold, 925 sterling silver, brass), gemstones, stone sizes & setting, finish, etc. We can also add your brand name or logo to the jewellery pieces.

What are your terms of payment?

We accept bank transfer. For smaller orders, we accept PayPal. For ready stock pieces, our payment terms are 100% advance. For items that require production, our payment terms are 50% on order and 50% post production before dispatch. We will share the photographs before we request the balance payment.

How do you ship the goods and how long does it take to arrive?

We usually ship express with companies like FedEx, UPS, DHL and Aramex. Shipping time varies anytime between 4 to 10 working days depending on the country, shipping agency and time taken for customs clearance. Our Customs House Agents are highly experienced in jewellery export procedures.

What is the production timeline?

Ready stock shipments can be shipped immediately. For items that require production, it can take anywhere between 7 to 25 working days. For custom designs, development time depends on the design — the minimum time required would be 3–4 working days.
